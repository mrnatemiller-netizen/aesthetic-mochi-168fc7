Fair comparison, not false balance.

The Divide is designed to make political coverage easier to understand—not to pretend every claim is equally supported.

Editorial guardrails

  • Label sources and their general editorial orientation transparently.
  • Distinguish reporting, opinion, advocacy, and commentary.
  • Show evidence and primary sources where possible.
  • Do not give unsupported claims equal weight with well-established facts.
  • Highlight uncertainty and genuine points of agreement.
