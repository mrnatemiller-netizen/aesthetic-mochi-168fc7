Sources & method

Feeds are retrieved from publishers through a public RSS-to-XML proxy and parsed in your browser. Each item links to the publisher's original story. Feed availability and refresh timing depend on publishers and the proxy.

Perspective labels are editorial-orientation descriptions, not truth ratings. Outlet placement does not mean every article shares the same viewpoint. We include mainstream, wire-service, public-media, and opinion-spectrum sources; reporting and opinion are not interchangeable.

This release is a live headline reader, not an AI-generated story matcher or fact-checker. It does not claim that two articles cover the exact same event. Headlines are not a substitute for reading the full reporting.
