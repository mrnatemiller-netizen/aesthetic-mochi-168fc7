Sources & method

Publisher RSS feeds are collected by a Netlify function and grouped heuristically by headline similarity. AI summaries are generated on request from headline and feed metadata only in this demo. They may miss nuance; read the original articles before drawing conclusions.

Perspective labels describe outlet tendencies, not the truth or bias of an individual story. We avoid treating opinions and factual reporting as equivalent. AI outputs are not fact-checks and may be wrong.

For full-text article summaries, we'd need licensed/accessible article text and should respect publishers' rights and terms.

About The Divide

The Divide helps readers compare coverage and framing without telling them what to think. It separates reporting, interpretation, and opinion—and encourages checking original sources.
